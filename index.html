Questions

Before you ask Galos. FAQ answers are drafts: confirm them in 07_content/Page — FAQ and 05_offer.

Are the workers people?+

No. Every worker is an AI, run by Galos. It always says so, and a person at Galos sets it up and watches it.

How long until it's live?+

About 7 days from the day you pay. Each worker's page shows its own days.

What do I need to give you?+

A few answers about your business, your prices and hours, and access to the accounts it works in. Never passwords in a chat.

Hire or Buy?+

Hire: Galos runs it and sends you the numbers every month. Buy: we set it up and it's yours to run.

Can I cancel?+

Hired workers: any month.
